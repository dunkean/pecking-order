# Pecking Order

Standalone knowledge base and deterministic resolver. Do not import sibling projects.
Keep generic institutional models separate from cultural bindings. A religion, country,
continent or language alone never establishes a title or a power structure.
Every cultural claim needs a scoped source; generic design choices must remain labelled.
Ship French and English labels and descriptions, preserving original-language titles.
Language keys are extensible BCP 47 tags; identifiers never depend on a translation.
Run `python -m pytest`, `ruff check src tests tools`, and `python -m pecking_order validate`.
After packaging changes, check a wheel outside the checkout. No credentials, environments,
generated worlds or downloaded source texts in Git. Never silently fabricate missing data.
